# Nimbus Cloud Storage — Product Knowledge Base

## About Nimbus
Nimbus Cloud Storage is a fictional file-sync-and-share product used for demo purposes in this project. Replace the contents of the `data/` folder with your own `.txt`, `.md`, or `.pdf` files to build a chatbot over your own knowledge base.

## Pricing Plans
- **Free**: 5 GB storage, 1 device, community support.
- **Plus**: 200 GB storage, 5 devices, email support. $4.99/month.
- **Pro**: 2 TB storage, unlimited devices, priority support, file versioning for 180 days. $9.99/month.
- **Business**: 5 TB per user, admin console, SSO, audit logs. $15/user/month.

## Key Features
1. **Real-time sync** across desktop, mobile, and web clients.
2. **File versioning**: Plus keeps 30 days of history, Pro keeps 180 days.
3. **Shared folders** with granular permissions (view, comment, edit).
4. **End-to-end encryption** is available on the Pro and Business tiers only.
5. **Offline access**: any file can be pinned for offline availability.

## Account & Billing FAQ
- To cancel a subscription, go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan. Access continues until the end of the current billing cycle.
- Refunds are issued for annual plans cancelled within 14 days of purchase.
- Storage overages on the Free plan pause uploads until space is freed up; no files are deleted automatically.
- You can downgrade a plan at any time, but if your usage exceeds the new plan's limit you'll be asked to remove files first.

## Security
Nimbus uses AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled under Settings > Security. Business plans support SAML-based SSO and can enforce 2FA org-wide.

## Support Channels
- Free & Plus: community forum and email support (48h response time).
- Pro: priority email support (12h response time).
- Business: dedicated account manager and 24/7 chat support.
